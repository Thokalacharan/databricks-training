# 📊 Week 4 • Day 6 | Advanced PySpark Mini Project – Employee Salary Analytics

> *Building a real-world employee salary analytics solution using advanced PySpark DataFrame operations and Window Functions.*

---

## 🎯 Objective

The objective of this mini project is to apply PySpark DataFrame transformations, aggregation functions, and Window Functions to analyze employee salary data and generate meaningful business insights.

| File                         | Description                                                                                                                         |

---

## 🚀 Key Features Implemented

### 🔹 Salary Analysis

* Identified the top three highest-paid employees in each department.
* Calculated the average salary for employees in every city.
* Classified employees into Low, Medium, and High salary categories.

### 🔹 Business Reports

* Generated salary reports based on employee joining year.
* Calculated department-wise average salaries.
* Identified employees earning higher than their department's average salary.

### 🔹 Advanced Analytics

* Applied `dense_rank()` for employee ranking.
* Used Window Functions with `partitionBy()` for department-level analysis.
* Combined aggregations with analytical reporting to generate business insights.
